Chain Stones

Blue 0Red 0

How to play
  • Take turns placing a stone from your hand on an empty square. Every side of a stone has 0 to 3 spikes.
  • Clash: each side of your new stone that touches an enemy stone fights it. Facing spikes break off one for one. The side with spikes left over wins and takes the other stone. That works both ways: a weak side against a strong one gets your new stone taken.
  • Chains: a stone you take is chained to the stone that took it, and chained stones always have the same owner. If the enemy takes any stone in a chain, the whole chain goes over to them.
  • When the board is full, count your stones on the board plus any still in your hand. Most stones wins. On 4 × 4 the first player gets a spare stone, because the second player places last.
  • Pick a stone, then hover over a square (or tap it once on a phone): yellow stones you would take, red stones you would lose, faded spikes would break.
