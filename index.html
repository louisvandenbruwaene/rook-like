Chain Stones

Blue 0Red 0

How to play
  • Take turns placing a stone from your hand on an empty square. Every side of a stone has 0 to 3 swords.
  • If a side of your new stone has more swords than the side of an enemy stone it touches, you take that stone.
  • Chains: a stone you take is chained to the stone that took it, and chained stones always have the same owner. If the enemy takes any stone in a chain, the whole chain goes over to them and joins the stone that took it. Chains only grow, so a big chain is a big prize.
  • When the board is full, count your stones on the board plus any still in your hand. Most stones wins.
  • Pick a stone, then hover over a square to see which stones you would take.
