How to play
  • A run is 20 matches. Lose one and the run is over. Match 20 is the boss.
  • Take turns placing a stone from your hand on an empty square of the 4 × 4 board. Your stones face up the board, the opponent's face down. You never see the opponent's hand.
  • Every stone is a chess piece. Each of its directions has spikes, one per level (up to 5).
  • Clash: your new stone fights every enemy stone it reaches or that reaches it. Facing spikes break off one for one, and the side with spikes left over wins and takes the other stone. A side with no spikes always loses to one with spikes. Lose any clash and your new stone is taken.
  • Your own stones block lines. When the board is full, count your stones. The dude with crown counts as 3. Most points wins. A draw is replayed.
  • After a win, pick one of three upgrades for the rest of the run. When a run ends, keep one stone you upgraded: it stays in your starting hand for good.
  • Every opponent always brings the same stones, and they get stronger as the run goes on. Remember what they play.
  • Pick a stone, then hover over a square (or tap it once on a phone): yellow stones you would take, red stones you would lose, faded spikes would break. Slow-mo (or S) shows every clash as a score.
